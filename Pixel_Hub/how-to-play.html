Learn the Arcade

How to Play Pixel Hub.

Every Pixel Hub game has a different goal, but getting started follows the same pattern: choose a game, press Play, watch the countdown, focus on the game window, and use the controls listed here.

This guide covers all six current games: Flappy Bird-style, Snake, Dino, Pong, 2048, and Breakout. Use it as a quick reference before playing or whenever you forget a control.

Starting any game

Pixel Hub uses one shared game window. You choose the game from the Games page, then that game takes control of the Canvas and its keyboard controls.

1. Choose Open the Games page and find the game you want.
2. Press Play The game window opens for that game and shows the countdown.
3. Control Use the keyboard, or pointer controls where supported.
4. Restart Use Restart Game below the Canvas when you want another attempt.
🐦 Flappy Bird-style

Keep the bird flying.

Each flap pushes the bird upward. Gravity pulls it back down, so the challenge is choosing the right time to flap while moving through the gaps.

Space ↑ W Click / Tap
  • Pass safely between the obstacles.
  • Do not hit the pipes or the edge of the play area.
  • Use short, controlled flaps instead of pressing constantly.
Tip: Watch the next opening, not just the bird. Small corrections are usually easier to control.
🐍 Snake

Eat apples. Keep growing.

Guide the snake around the grid. Every apple makes the snake longer, which means the available space becomes harder to manage.

↑ ↓ ← → W A S D
  • Eat apples to increase your score and length.
  • Avoid the outside walls.
  • Do not run the snake into its own body.
Tip: Leave yourself open paths. Tight loops can trap a long snake quickly.
🦖 Dino

Jump and keep running.

The Dino moves forward automatically. Your job is to time each jump so it clears cactus groups and flying pterosaurs as the game gradually gets faster.

Space ↑ W
  • Jump over ground obstacles.
  • Watch for flying obstacles as the run continues.
  • The speed increases as you progress.
Tip: Do not jump too early. Keep enough room to land before the next obstacle arrives.
🏓 Pong

Beat the CPU to 7.

Move your paddle up and down to return the ball. The computer controls the opposite paddle, and the first side to reach 7 points wins.

W / S ↑ / ↓ 1 Easy 2 Medium 3 Hard 4 Impossible
  • Keep your paddle lined up with the incoming ball.
  • Use number keys 1–4 to change CPU difficulty.
  • Score when the ball passes the CPU paddle.
Tip: Hitting the ball away from the center of your paddle can help create harder return angles.
🔢 2048

Combine matching tiles.

Every move slides the whole board in one direction. Tiles with the same value combine when they meet, creating a larger tile.

↑ ↓ ← → W A S D
  • Combine equal-number tiles such as 2 + 2 or 16 + 16.
  • Try to keep enough empty spaces for future moves.
  • The main goal is to create the 2048 tile.
Tip: Keeping your largest tiles near one corner can make the board easier to organize.
🧱 Breakout

Clear the bricks and survive the levels.

Move the paddle underneath the ball so it keeps bouncing into the bricks. New levels use different brick formations and the ball becomes faster as you progress.

A / D ← / →
  • You begin with 3 lives.
  • Break every brick to reach the next level.
  • Catch falling power-ups for +100 points, an extra life, a wider paddle, a slower ball, or multiball.
  • The brick pattern changes and the ball speed increases on later levels.
Tip: Keep the paddle near the ball's projected landing point. Chasing the ball too late is much harder at higher speeds.
